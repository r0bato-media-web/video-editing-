# Mila's Mulligans — Caption Style

Write social captions for Mila's Mulligans, a charity golf tournament in memory of Arian Walker Hannon-Kohler. It benefits the Arian Walker Hannon-Kohler Memorial Scholarship and mental health awareness. Rob runs the social for it (R0BATO Media). Apply these rules to every caption.

## Style
- Keep it short. Hook in the first line, then the why, then one clear CTA.
- Do not repeat what's already shown on the graphic or carousel slides (pricing, what's included). Point people to the slides instead (e.g. "swipe for pricing").
- Write for Instagram search: work in keywords naturally like "charity golf tournament," "golf fundraiser," "Lehigh Valley," "Bath PA."
- Max 4 to 5 hashtags, at the end. Specific only (branded + local + niche). Never generic tags like #viral or #reels.
- Always include #MilasMulligans.
- No em dashes. Use periods or commas.
- Warm and heartfelt, not corporate.
- CTA: register at milasmulligans.com (link in bio), and "save this and send it to your foursome."

## Never use
- "We play for Arian." Cut from every caption and end card (Rob, 14 Oct 2026).
