- Angel's "about 80 golfers" (angel 74.2-77.6). It's one golfer's guess, and the player count has no source.
- First names heard on the clips ("Angel", "Tristan") as on-screen labels. Names come from the client, never from a transcript.

Done means:
- Every guess checked by mic and mouth, one per person, at full resolution.
- The total on screen matches its source word for word, and the source is listed in the sidecar.
- Same checks as reel 2: frames every 0.25 s, sync within 2 ms, -14 LUFS, no frozen frames before the end card.
